## Assessment

### What must become true

1. The qualified candidate semantic/reference tree and the protected-only governance/source-boundary corrections coexist on one exact acceptance subject.
2. Proposal contracts become one coherent accepted semantic authority with exact version/identity/status and no unresolved TBD meaning.
3. Search IR 0.2, normalizers, Composer products, requirement coverage, registry, status and control state identify the same accepted contract set.
4. #193's ownership correction is enforced before the proposal execution-package/program-profile surface can become accepted authority.
5. Full repository/reference validation passes on the exact final head.
6. Protected `main` remains unchanged until exact-head review, required checks and guarded merge; production/native work remains blocked until protected readback.

### Strongest adverse finding

The current proposal `execution-package.schema.json` and `program-package-profile.schema.json` encode CUDA-JS-shaped request vocabulary locally: `kernel/device` function kinds, Device-JS parameter types, `device-memory`, generic access values, launch geometry/shared-memory/pending-operation fields, and a mixed compile/allocate/load/admit/ignite/cancel/complete/teardown lifecycle.

These shapes were valid bounded proposal/reference evidence, but accepting them unchanged would make CUDA-MCGS a competing normative definition of CUDA-JS request validity and lower resource lifecycle. That contradicts ADR-0014/0019 and the protected #193 audit.

### Required field dispositions

Every lower-facing package field must be classified exactly as one of:

1. genuine MCGS semantic/resource requirement — retain;
2. MCGS selected-profile policy over a generic lower primitive — retain;
3. adapter translation validated by the selected public CUDA-JS contract — retain as MCGS-to-lower mapping evidence, not lower authority;
4. opaque/mechanically lower-bound value — bind to exact lower contract/capability identity;
5. copied lower fact — remove from MCGS authority.

Selected MCGS geometry/topology policy may remain explicit. CUDA-JS owns validity, limits, primitive vocabulary, provider facts, compiler/resource/operation mechanics and lifecycle.

## Falsifiers / stop conditions

Stop and revise rather than force acceptance if any of these occur:

- a protected-only authority/source-boundary change is lost;
- a proposal field remains an independent normative copy of CUDA-JS validity/lifecycle/provider facts;
- changing contract status/identity exposes an unclassified or stale generated route;
- any required owner/reference case is missing/skipped or a mutation family no longer fails;
- product/chess/ranking/model assumptions enter universal meaning;
- a host progress loop, native/private CUDA-MCGS path, physical scheduler topology or unsupported lower contract becomes required to make the packet coherent;
- accepted-spec/index/contract-set/registry/status/control-state identities disagree;
- branch/head changes after review without review invalidation and requalification.

## Rollback / safe stop

Protected `main` is not mutated during construction. The acceptance branch may be abandoned without changing protected authority. `experimental/portfolio` remains historical qualified evidence and is not deleted. No production/native issue is unblocked until protected #122 readback.
